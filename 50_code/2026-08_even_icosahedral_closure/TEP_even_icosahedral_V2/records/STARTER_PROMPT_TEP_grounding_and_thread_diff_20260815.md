### 1.2 The stamp vocabulary — learn it before you use it

Every claim in this corpus is tiered, and the tier is part of the claim. Do not restate a claim without its tier.

- **[T]** derived/proved here · **[T-cited]** a theorem from the literature, applied · **[V]** verified this session (machine-checked) · **[C]** consonance / structural correspondence, not proof · **[O]** open question, named · **[A]** frame or analogy, explicitly priced as such · **[id]** identification held at canon weight · **[recon]** reconstructed from structure with the source unread — permitted *only* where no pointer exists, and always labelled.
- **certified-numerical** — measured to stated precision on stated instruments; not proof. Most of the strongest results in the Langlands arc live here, and they are careful to say so.
- **pun-tier** — a linguistic or notational rhyme with no mechanism behind it. The corpus treats pun-detection as a first-class duty. Assume any unpriced correspondence you find is a pun until you locate its mechanism.

### 3.1 How to tell the threads apart

At least two lineages ran in parallel through this arc, sometimes on the same day. Forensic markers, roughly reliable:

- **Filename suffix.** A trailing `B` on a date (`…20260809B`, `…20260813B`) marks one lineage; unlettered same-date files mark the other. This convention was adopted *after* a collision in which one session's consolidation entry overwrote another's.
- **Self-identification in the header italics** ("the assessor session" vs "this session"), and which prior records each cites as kin versus as its own.
- **Instrument fingerprints.** One thread worked in float64 against a 97-prime campaign CSV with box-bisection tilings; the other worked at mpmath dps 28–40 against the 9,592-prime cleanroom tables with winding-plus-census accounting.
- **Prediction-naming schemes.** Letter-prefixed families (E-a…E-d, Z-a…Z-e, M-a…M-c, P-t1, Q-t2, H-a…H-c) versus numbered families (P1–P5, V-a…V-c).
- **Countersign status.** One lineage stayed offered-not-self-filed throughout; the other had countersign suspended by Will on 2026-08-11 and filed under that suspension.

Where a thread cannot be identified, say so and treat the record as unattributed rather than guessing.

### 3.2 The diagnostic you are actually running

For each substantive result in the arc, ask **three questions in this order**:

1. **What external apparatus is doing the work?** (Booker, Brauer, Langlands–Tunnell, Deligne–Langlands, Khare–Wintenberger, Jacquet–Shalika, Selberg/Eisenstein theory, Petz/Chentsov, Helgason/Cormack, Slepian, Wiener–Khinchin, fractal uncertainty, random-wave heuristics…)

3. **Was that native object used as machinery, or only as decoration?** Machinery *computes* — it changes a number, forces a prediction, or supplies a step. Decoration re-describes a result already fully obtained by external means.

**The failure mode you are hunting is a result whose answer to (2) exists but whose answer to (3) is "decoration" — where a TEP-native route was available and would have gotten there faster, further, or with an extra consequence, and was passed over because the external apparatus was closer to hand.**
