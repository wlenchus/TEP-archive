## A. Why present-first

Reading order is not historical order. V2's spine (like V1's, and the 07-23 README's) walked a new reader chronologically: v0.5's superseded formalism first, the current framework twenty documents later, the errata somewhere in the middle. That ordering optimizes for provenance and costs exactly what Will named: rigor-presentation and confidence. A stranger's first contact was the program's weakest, most-corrected stratum — and they met it *before* acquiring the correction apparatus, which is how "spurious open problems" and sunsetted formalism get re-absorbed as live.

V3 inverts it. The correction apparatus is the **gate** — nothing else is read without it. Then the framework as it currently stands, then the current evidence, then the how-we-know layer, and only then the historical strata, explicitly labeled *provenance, not doctrine*. Nothing is removed; everything before Stratum 3 is post-May-2026 material. The archive keeps its history; the reader no longer starts in it.
