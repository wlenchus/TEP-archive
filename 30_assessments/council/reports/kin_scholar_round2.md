### Q3 (dictionary) — what is the practice layer *for*, post-Jin?

Answered seriously, in five parts, after verifying the Jin ecology (§2 below).

1. **Jin's win was itself practice-layer-shaped.** The Townsend–Greenbaum SIAM News account [wv] shows the "16-hour autonomous run" was autonomous in *execution* only. Jin's design: **web access explicitly denied** (a contamination control), a forced "branching portfolio of genuinely different approaches" with independent strategy subagents (anti-premature-convergence), **in-run adversarial audits with counterexample testing**, and a completion gate ("not to give up until a complete proof survived checking"). The amateur who beat Crouzeix did it by building a miniature verification discipline *into* the run. Capability didn't beat instrumentation; instrumented capability beat the conjecture.
2. **Trust is the product; capability only makes candidates.** Jin's preprint became mathematics through: public artifact (GitHub + preprint) → expert engagement within hours-to-days (Townsend, Greenbaum, Michel Crouzeix — all endorse) → an independent professional proof 8 days later. Townsend's own worry is the flood: *"We do not yet know whether academia has the capacity to absorb the flood of results that may be coming."* In a flood, verification attention is the scarce resource, and the differentiator is **arriving pre-verified** — methods, provenance, honest bounds attached. That is what the practice layer manufactures, and the deposit is its shipping dock. Post-Jin, the layer's price went **up**, not down.
3. **Comparative advantage concentrates on discovery-provenance.** For proofs of *known statements* (Crouzeix), weights-contamination is irrelevant — validity is checkable regardless of how it was found. For *new-object discovery* (the corpus's decodes and datasets), provenance **is** the result: without the disclosure→seal→commit→grade chain, "new" is unprovable in a world where the model may have seen everything. Proofs-of-known-statements commoditize first; provenanced new objects commoditize last. The corpus's asset class is the second. The practice layer is what keeps it an asset.
4. **It converts capability bets into information — and post-Jin it licenses a new bet.** The corrected tally shows the prediction column occupied-and-honestly-killed under prereg (E-d/P-t1): failures became data. The Jin datapoint (universal-bound mode now autonomously reachable at least once — U-K2) licenses a **preregistered Jin-mode attempt on the one-power criterion**: strongest available model; Jin-style harness (denied external context, branching portfolio, adversarial audit subagents, survive-checking gate); kill conditions fixed before the run. Honest scoping: Crouzeix is self-contained analysis over a rich classical literature; the one-power criterion sits on a bespoke object with thin literature — retrieval-poor cuts both ways (less contamination risk, less support). A lottery ticket — but prereg makes either outcome citable, which is the practice layer's whole trick. Strictly post-deposit.
5. **What it is not for.** Not a moat (capability rises regardless); not a certifier (Q1 — its in-house review is defect-finding; countersign→deposit→experts remains the only certification path); and not self-maintaining — assessor's hash-discipline drift (SHA-256 real through 08-04, absent after, both lineages) shows the layer thinning exactly as the results strengthened. The flood era rewards the opposite gradient.

**One-line answer:** pre-Jin the practice layer was how limited capability got squeezed into trustworthy results; post-Jin it is how abundant capability gets distinguished from noise. Same instrument, higher price.
