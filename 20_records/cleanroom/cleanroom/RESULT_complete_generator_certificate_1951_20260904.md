## 3. What this is and is not
It is the strongest pointwise statement the instrument can make: the candidate built from the Galois-side table is invariant, at the tested points and to the stated floors, under a *complete* generating set of Γ₀(1951) — not three generators but all of them — with the elliptic torsion generators included. T-periodicity is built in. **Fricke covariance is not a theorem for this candidate** ⟨corrected 09-04, assessor-adversary handshake finding⟩: turning the Brauer functional equation into Cauchy data on the imaginary axis requires shifting the Mellin contour across the strip, which picks up a residue term Σ Res·y^{1−ρ₀} at every pole of Λ — the data vanish iff Λ is entire, so Fricke covariance ⟺ entirety ⟺ (Booker 2003) the conjecture. It was certified at 10⁻²⁶ on 08-09; the 326 generators tested here plus Fricke are the conjecture-content, all at instrument grade. The first version of this sentence promoted an instrument reading to a theorem in one hop. It is not a proof: a pointwise identity at finitely many points, and the large-m generators are floor-limited because the symmetric point sits at height 1/(mN) where the Fourier series needs more coefficients than the table holds. Instrument class; certifies the object, not the conjecture.
