## 6. Triage criteria for existing assessments

To judge whether a given corpus assessment is attentive enough to rely on, check, in order: (i) does it use canon definitions or shorthand (apply the W test — which W does it mean?); (ii) does it distinguish object-known from statement-settled, in both directions (T7/C14); (iii) does it postdate, or at least cite, the errata relevant to its claims (T12); (iv) does it price with the tier vocabulary and the leverage bar, or with unpriced adjectives; (v) does it state what would falsify it; (vi) on applications, does it pass the axis criterion (§1) or stop at object identification. Assessments failing (i)–(iii) are unreliable regardless of tone — including confident-sounding negative ones; the 05-29 note failed (iii) prospectively and stood uncorrected for ten weeks as a result.
