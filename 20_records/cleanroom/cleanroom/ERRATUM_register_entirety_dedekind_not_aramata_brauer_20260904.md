## Why it is wrong
Aramata–Brauer proves ζ_L/ζ_k entire for **Galois** L/k. K₅/ℚ (Klein's quintic, closure group A₅) is not Galois. Entirety of ζ_{K₅}/ζ is **Dedekind's conjecture** at K₅, proved when the Galois closure is solvable (Uchida 1975; van der Waall 1975) and **not** for A₅. No Brauer–Hecke route exists either: A₅ has no subgroup of index 2, 3 or 4, so the 4-dimensional character has no non-negative monomial expression — only ratios, e.g. χ₄ = Ind_{A₄}1 − 1 = Ind_{S₃}1 − Ind_{D₅}1 = Ind_{V₄}χ − Ind_{D₅}sgn − Ind_{A₄}ω, giving ζ_{K₅}/ζ = ζ_{K₁₀}/ζ_{K₆} = L_{K₁₅}(χ̃)/(L_{K₆}(sgn)·L_{K₅}(ω̃)): meromorphic, poles confined to zeros of the denominators, entirety unproven.

- Mode-(b) "tensor flatness" presupposes automorphic factors, which is the conjecture; the register is mode-(b)-**shaped**, flat **iff** the 2-dims are automorphic. The exact two-tiles of the closure record (L(ρ̃)L(ρ̃⊗χ₄) and L(ρ̃)L(ρ̃⊗χ₅) entire, via automorphy over K₅ and K₆) are unaffected.

The register is the most expensive face; its entirety alone descends to nothing.
