## 3. What "decisive" has actually required — and the deposit

Common denominators of every claim that *stuck* (JC counterexample, AlphaEvolve items, #728, First Proof solves), against every claim that deflated (GPT-5 Oct 2025, OpenAI IMO 2025 process, #333 first attempt):

1. **A machine-checkable artifact** — explicit construction, symbolic certificate, or Lean proof. Every 2026 decisive result is certificate-shaped.
2. **Independent re-implementation** — two separately implemented verifiers (Thompson's JC form), public verification repos (AlphaEvolve), fresh-instance checks (Erdős practice per Quanta).
3. **Human digestion** — a conceptual account by mathematicians (Sawin/Levinson's geometry for JC; Gowers/Tsimerman commentary for #90). Tao's essay elevates this to the community's scarce resource.
4. **Public deposit within days** — gists, repos, arXiv. The deflations all lacked disclosed methods or inspectable artifacts at announcement time; Ochigame's criticism of OpenAI (#90) is precisely "methods, prompts, compute undisclosed."

**Mapping to the Langlands arc** [doc+inf]: the corpus already has (1) in unusual strength (exact ℤ[ζ₅] construction, certified numerics with stated floors, SHA-256 manifests, md5-stamped keys, OpenTimestamps suggestion) and (2) in-house (blind replication, fresh-code-path maass verification) — but its (2) is same-family (Claude checking Claude; the r4 record says so itself). It has essentially none of (3)–(4) externally. Both route through the **same action: the Zenodo/arXiv deposit**, which the protocol already ranks #1 and which was untouched as of 08-15. The external record's lesson is unambiguous and current: *vendor-style claims without deposit sit in limbo even with Lean certificates (Astra, three weeks unconfirmed); deposited artifacts become canon within weeks even from amateurs.* The deposit package should include the prereg chain, seals, and the training-prior disclosure — exactly the material whose absence the community faulted OpenAI for. The corpus is, unusually, *already deposit-shaped*; the remaining cost is human-time, not method.

## 7. Calibration counsel for the council

1. **Council consensus is same-model consensus.** Six Fable 5 seats agreeing is one witness with six voices — the r4 same-family caveat applies to *us*. Treat cross-seat convergence as drafting quality, not verification.
2. Trust the council on: audit, derivation-inside-committed-structure, literature triage, certificate construction and checking, adversarial review. Distrust on: unverified forward assertion, novelty claims without a search, self-rated confidence (Tao's line), and anything where fluency in external apparatus can outrun the native machinery (the corpus's documented ports-vs-modes failure — caught by Will, twice).
3. **Insist on external verification exactly where the field does**: machine-checkable artifact → independent re-implementation (ideally non-Claude toolchain: Magma/Sage/PARI, already named in r4) → human digestion → deposit. The deposit is not filing hygiene; in 2026 it is the difference between a result existing and not existing.
4. Where a claim can be made certificate-shaped, make it so before arguing about it (external lesson: repair rounds work when a verifier names defects; assertion volume does not).
