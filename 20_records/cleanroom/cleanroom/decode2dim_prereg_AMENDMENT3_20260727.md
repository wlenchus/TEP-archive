# AMENDMENT 3 to the 2-dim decode pre-registration — successive-cancellation decoding — 2026-07-27 (~17:00Z)

**Trigger (measured):** under the port-native meter objective (Amendment 2), the structured start at the leading cell (odd, a=2, ε=+1) improved 11× to 1.31e-5, but the 4-start random battery still scatters (4.1e-5–5.5e-4) with no consensus. Diagnosis from the measured structure: with 2260 bits, the flip-signal of a prime with kernel weight w_p is unreadable while heavier undecided bits contribute noise ≫ w_p — greedy from arbitrary inits cannot cascade into the lower digits. The e-5 wall is the aggregate noise floor of the undecided mid-band, not an information limit: the certified instruments resolve single-coefficient errors at 1e-14, and ~30–50 arm-values at ~15 digits carry ~10³ bits of information — enough in principle, extractable only in weight order.

**Amended decoder (answer-free, specified before first run):** successive cancellation. (i) All bit-primes start **marginalized**: local inverse-series = the bit-average of the two candidate inverse factors — exactly the even part of (1 ∓ m_pT + T²)^{-1}, which is bit-independent; odd noise halved, even content exact. (ii) Primes are decided one at a time in **decreasing exact signature weight** (norm of the prime's arm-signature column, machine-computed), each decision made by evaluating both bit values with heavier bits frozen-as-decided and lighter bits still marginalized; decision feedback proceeds down the ladder. (iii) Polish: the Amendment-2 greedy + pair pass on the full decided vector. (iv) Consensus battery: ≥3 SC runs with perturbed decision order must agree on the sensitivity-visible set. Internal decode oracle: the port meter on a **widened u-grid** (30 points, u ∈ [0.02, 0.40]; X scaled to keep tail ≤ 1e-15). **Certification metrics are unchanged**: the pre-registered 7-point theta residual and 7-point meter, the blind N-scan, the negative battery, and all §7 bars — the widened grid is decoder-internal only, so the goalposts do not move.

**No other change.** If SC also fails to reach the bars, the result is reported as KD-2/KD-3 with the full measured trail (theta-greedy scatter, meter-greedy scatter, SC outcome) — three independently-specified decoders failing at a consistent floor would itself be a finding about the corner's readability at PoC fidelity, and would be priced as such, not hidden.
