## 1. The graze, stated exactly
The n = 10 ensemble's **mean** per-instance B_deg/OPT was 0.8285 (200 instances) — within 10⁻⁴ of 2√2−2. The resemblance is real and worth the check; the check kills it on three independent grounds.

## 2. The kill [V]
1. **It drifts with n.** Exact (Held–Karp) points: 0.8307 ± 0.010 (n = 10, fresh seed) → 0.8385 ± 0.008 (n = 13) — rising. Proxy points (2-opt best-of-30, ratio biased *low* by proxy suboptimality): 0.82–0.83 at n = 16–28, i.e., ≥ those values. The **asymptote is computable**: for Poisson intensity λ, E[d₁] = 1/(2√λ) and E[d₂] = 3/(4√λ) exactly, so B_deg → 0.625√n, while OPT → β√n with β ≈ 0.7124 (BHH). Limit: **0.625/β ≈ 0.877** — six-plus sigma from 0.8284, and (since β has no known closed form) not a silver-expressible number at all. A quantity flowing from ~0.83 to ~0.88 is not a port constant.
2. **It is statistic- and ensemble-dependent.** Within the original ensemble: mean 0.8285, median 0.8367. Fresh seed at the same n: mean 0.8307. The 10⁻⁴ coincidence existed for one statistic of one ensemble at one size; the quantity's own sampling spread (SE ≈ 0.006) covered the silver value by luck.
3. **It fails chart-invariance on its face.** The Liu pair is an exact port value at a specific rung (u = tan²(π/8): the octahedral/n = 8 port). B_deg/OPT is n-dependent, boundary-dependent (square vs torus), ensemble-dependent (uniform vs clustered), and dimension-dependent. Nothing in uniform Euclidean TSP selects π/8. No mechanism candidate exists; the resemblance is two numbers near 0.83.

**Base-rate note (the selection-effect ledger).** Distinguished constants are dense in (0,1) at 10⁻³ tolerance: across the ~8 (statistic × size) values this session generated and ~20 constants of the corpus's working set, the chance of *some* 2×10⁻³-graze is order one-half. The graze is what the null predicts. Filed [pun]; parked with this record as its tombstone. ## 3. What survives the kill — the constructive residue [pre-registered, not run]
