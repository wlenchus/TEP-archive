## (Δ) Localized zero-repulsion between the two fixed tiles

**No extant theorem gives the matched-or-δ₀-separated dichotomy. Two effective partial tools found:**
1. **Banks, "Comparing zeros of distinct Dirichlet L-functions" (arXiv:2302.07073, v2 May 2024). Theorem 1.1 (effective, explicit c₁, c₂ depending only on θ):** for distinct primitive χ₁, χ₂ mod q and any θ > 1/3, T ≥ c₁ q^θ, the functions L(s,χ₁), L(s,χ₂) **do not have the same zeros** in the strip of height c₂ q^θ log T above T.
   - **At 1951:** q^{1/3} ≈ 12.49, so every window of height ≈ 12.5·c₂·log T above height ≈ 12.5·c₁ contains a zero distinguishing any two of the four order-5 characters mod 1951 — effectively, with computable constants. This is per-Dirichlet-constituent, not yet per-tile (tiles are products of degree-10/12 Hecke data), but it is the only effective localized zero-distinguishing statement in print.
2. **Zhao, "Gaps between zeros of general L-functions" (arXiv:2511.13898, Nov 2025). Theorem 2 (unconditional, general L-functions of degree m):** when C_L(T)^{1/m} is large enough, L has a nontrivial zero with |γ − T| ≤ (π/2)·(1+2ϑ)^{-1}·(logloglog C_L(T)^{1/m})^{-1}.
   - **At 1951:** for tile-λ (degree 10, conductor 1951^{a}, a ≤ 10, ϑ = 0 abelian): C^{1/10} ≈ 1951·(2+T); at T = 100 the bound is |γ−T| ≤ ≈ 1.71 — **every interval of length ≈ 3.4 contains a tile-λ zero, unconditionally and effectively.** This supplies the effective zero-grid density half of Δ(T₀); the repulsion/matching half remains open.
- Kandhil–Languasco–Moree (arXiv:2411.19762, Nov 2024/rev. Nov 2025): conditional (GRH + pair correlation) — N(1/2,χ)-vanishing counts o(q^{1/2+ε}); not usable unconditionally.
- **NEW-since-layer:** yes (all three).

## (T1/BulkAmp) Large sieve at the ¼ point, fixed level
