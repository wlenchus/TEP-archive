# The TEP Archive

**The Terminal Equivalence Principle (TEP)** — a boundary-first, operationalist research program in mathematical physics: the physical content of a passive system is taken to be exhaustively characterized by its boundary response (its Dirichlet-to-Neumann operator), with the Schur complement as the fundamental operation of physical reduction. From that stance, across August 2025 – September 2026, the corpus develops an information-geometric **budget** formalism (x² + u = 1, with its gain, meter and port vocabulary; the budget derived twice and the Fisher metric forced at the metric-space level modulo an itemized remainder — Foundations Status v2); tiered-rigor **theorem notes** on the publication track (the purity/decoherence speed limit with constructive saturation, the homogeneity-closure/monotone-metrics note, the Schur–TEP lemma bundle, the scale-shape decomposition and its well-posedness closure); an **experimental dictionary** with its falsification record kept at full volume (thin films, the DHO/Butterworth register, Crouzeix, the Liu saga, the γ* nesting); a **transmission-functor** bridge from the budget to Langlands-type structures (June–July 2026: canon A7, the coding theorem, the reflection and dead-channel theorems, the OT1 decoder instrument, the carrier campaign and rung 4); and, from late July 2026, the **even-icosahedral campaign** — a seam-constraint decoding instrument that produced, and saw constructor-confirmed, Hecke-eigenvalue data for the Doud-1951 and Doud-2141 representations (no prior numerical treatment found as of 2026-08-26), the explicit Maass-form candidates built from them, and an attempt on the Artin conjecture for those two representations that is open and, by the record's own September verdict, not closable with the present tools.

This repository is the **curated public record** of that program. It now holds roughly 800 primary documents, scripts, and data files spanning August 2025 – September 2026, selected and organized from a working corpus of 2,600+ Drive files plus a sealed project shelf. The July 2026 layer is indexed in [`CATALOG.csv`](CATALOG.csv) (through 2026-07-16) and [`2026-08-05 catalog_delta_master_ActiveV1_1.csv`](2026-08-05%20catalog_delta_master_ActiveV1_1.csv) (2026-07-16 → 08-05); the layer added on 2026-09-12 is indexed file-by-file, with SHA-256 hashes and provenance, in [`CATALOG_ADDENDUM_2026-07-25_to_2026-09-12.csv`](CATALOG_ADDENDUM_2026-07-25_to_2026-09-12.csv).

## Honest framing

The corpus's character judgment of record is the 2026-05-29 closing note: *not crankery, not a breakthrough* — a mathematically serious constraint theory of measurement geometry, with its verified results, retired claims and open questions kept on the record. The council's 2026-08-26 re-sign record states the program's present position in two registers — *classical machinery realizes the results; the framework selected them*: the artifacts (theorem notes, datasets, certificates) stand framework-free, which is the only arrangement under which they can count as evidence about TEP, while the framework's discovery provenance — why this grid, this constraint system, this ring, these primes — travels with them in a motivation register, and TEP's own candidacy as a physical theory stays separately priced and open. The record grades its own claim modes: derivations and audits have held; the prediction column is empty, not zero (08-09 re-read — the three falsified predictions were assessor-constructed extensions); the spine's retirement register (Spine V4 §F3, offered) runs in both directions — underclaims retired ("reorganization," "modest verified content," "axioms assumed"), false-opens rectified (metric selection, the ζ = 1/√2 mislabel, the Healing Conjecture already a 2020 theorem), and overclaims retired symmetrically (Liu priority zero, the thin-film layers, the γ* scope, W↔Lévy open by the author's own retraction).

A two-sided restamp of the 05-29 judgment was **proposed** on 2026-08-06 (`20_records/cleanroom/assessments/adjudication_closing_note_characterization_DraftV1_20260806.md` — offered, not countersigned as of 2026-08-26): it would retain *not crankery* and *methodologically exemplary*, and supersede "a reorganization with modest verified content" on the ground that the even-icosahedral Hecke datasets are first-of-their-kind arithmetic data — constructor-confirmed 65/65 per field modulo one pre-declared quadratic twist (E0(ii) sweep, five disclosed coverage gaps). Foundations are stated as forced at the metric-space level modulo an itemized remainder (O-a anchor provenance, O-d write-up, O-e counting, the compositionality postulate, the per-register clause — canon A7; Foundations Status block v2, `00_canon/2026-07-25 correctness_patch_bundle_ActiveV1.md`, appendix).

On the Artin conjecture itself the record is unambiguous, and the September 2026 council handshake made it unanimous: **not closed, and not equipped to close it decisively**; equipped to state it exactly, certify the object, and deposit. The Heilbronn coin of 2026-09-04 names the missing inequality and shows that no subgroup of 2.A₅ supplies it; every certificate in the record (pointwise automorphy at 10⁻²⁴, Fricke covariance at 10⁻²⁶, the 326- and 358-generator certificates) is an instrument reading of the conjecture's content, not a proof of it — Fricke covariance is equivalent to entirety, hence to the conjecture.

**None of this has been refereed or deposited.** That clause is the operative one and gates every campaign (direction memo 2026-07-25; adjudication 2026-08-06; council orientation 2026-08-26). The Zenodo/arXiv deposit is the program's own stated first priority and has not yet been made.

### The not-claimed box
Strong Artin for the even icosahedral representations at conductors 1951 and 2141 is not claimed. Entirety of L(ρ̃) is not claimed; entirety of ζ_{K₅}/ζ (Dedekind's conjecture at the A₅ quintic) is not claimed (erratum 2026-09-04). Automorphy of the constructed Maass candidates is not claimed — it is measured at finite precision. No result in this archive has been re-run by a person outside the collaboration. The framework's effectiveness is demonstrated on the readability side (parity read, explicit derivation, certified constants, exact moments) and on machine-checked identities; on the proof side it is undemonstrated by the record's own ledger.

## Countersign — what is signed, and how to read "offered"

The corpus's rule, corrected by Will and confirmed on 2026-08-26 (`COUNCIL_rulings_gammastar_countersign_attribution_20260826.md` §2; primaries `30_assessments/council/reports/archivist_countersign_reaudit.md`, `assessor_countersign_reaudit.md`): his signature is an **edit**, not a remark — the triad of in-place removal of the pre-countersign caveat (verified against the delivered text), a dated headline or stamp, and a version activation or increment; any one leg alone is not signature (an upload is filing, not signing; an increment can be session-authored). Under that rule the **Drive canon layer is signed**: the Central Reference → ActiveV3 and the interior spine trace → ActiveV12 (07-23, with the dated 07-22 P2 stamp), the session-delta family's increments, the June canon cluster's Active stamps — the canon is canonical because he edit-signed it, and the records before this layer that carry `ActiveVn` in `00_canon/` and `20_records/` are that register. The **project-arc records (2026-07-27 → 2026-08-15), the 08-06 adjudication draft, Spine V4, and the council's own records showed no triads as of 2026-08-26** and carry "offered, not self-filed" as their own label — their state, not a grade assigned here. The Drive copies of those records mirrored on 2026-09-12 differ from the project copies only by session-written headers (superseded-in-part notices, "canonical copy" notes). Convention going forward, adopted 08-26: a grep-safe `[countersigned YYYY-MM-DD — scope]` line; grep for the stamp, never infer signature from the absence of a caveat or its removal from the absence of a stamp.

## Conventions you'll see in the documents

`ActiveVn` = current working version · `ArchivalVn` = retained snapshot · `DraftVn` = working draft · `DeltaN` = incremental addendum · Tier 1–5 = the rigor ladder (Tier 1 verified theorem → Tier 5 retired claim) · `[V]` = claim with a reproducibility path (ledger patch P5 proposes that every [V] record end with "Scripts archived at:"; still outstanding) · `[T]` machine-checked / proved in-record · `[T-v]` proved by a prover session and verified with named repairs · `[T-cited]` a cited theorem (the 2026-09-04 citation-card rule requires source, theorem number and hypotheses verbatim at the point of use; earlier `[T-cited]` tags predate the rule and several were found applied outside their hypotheses) · `[Tsk]` skeleton-grade · `[CN]` certified-numerical · `[cal]` calibrated against controls · `[id]` an identification/relabeling, never load-bearing · `[R]` reading/dictionary layer · `[C]` conjectural identification · `[O]`/`[open]` named open statement · `[recalled]` rests on memory of a standard result, unpinned · **offered** = offered-not-self-filed, awaiting Will's countersign (the default state of every record since 2026-07-27) · **countersigned** = adjudicated stamp on record. Campaign records carry parity qualifiers ("odd-icosahedral (calibration)" vs "even-icosahedral (the corner)") and prediction-class labels (forced / pattern / no-prediction / novel). Council records label obstructions (M) method-relative, (L) language-invariant, or (A) audit findings (2026-09-04).

Four documents govern how to read everything else: the consolidated errata record (2026-06-10) with its successors (ledger patches ActiveV2; correctness patch bundle; the 2026-09-12 index); the review postmortem (2026-05-26) — "trust derivations, distrust consolidation prose," a rule that applies to this README; the countersign ledger for everything offered; and, for the closure campaign, the 2026-08-17 manifest (the precedence document: where a parent and a correction disagree, the correction wins; where anything disagrees with its canonical-values table, the table wins).

## Provenance & method

The July 2026 layer was assembled from the Drive corpus by an automated cataloging pass with per-document review (see the 07-16 and 08-05 catalogs). The September 2026 layer was assembled as follows. Project documents were mirrored byte-exactly from the claude.ai project and checked against the clean-room SHA-256 manifests where they list them (11/11 matching) and against the 2026-08-29 bundle manifest. Drive files were fetched through the Drive connector: as base64 with byte-length verification against Drive's file size (and zip CRC where applicable), and where a second, independently transcribed copy existed, cross-checked against it modulo whitespace; Google-Docs-native records were exported as text/markdown and are marked as exports in the catalog addendum (no byte-exact form exists for them). Every added file carries its SHA-256 and its source (project path, Drive file id, or session bundle) in `CATALOG_ADDENDUM_2026-07-25_to_2026-09-12.csv`. The clean-room layer's own integrity spine — append-only PROVENANCE_MANIFEST files (v1–v7) and the two deposit-bundle manifests — is mirrored unchanged; a single external timestamp on those manifests would pin the whole layer.
