## 2. Novelty verdict on the central construction (deliverable 2 — gates the deposit)

**Verdict: the corpus's E0ii sweep's NO survives my independent re-sweep.** I found no prior exhibition of an even icosahedral Maass form (as a function or as a coefficient table), no prior numerical automorphy/entirety test of an even icosahedral Artin L-function, and no computed zeros of one. Nearest prior art, delimited:

- **Doud–Moore 2006** [web]: the representation-side enumeration (the quintic and conductor the corpus imports, and discloses importing). No analytic data.
- **Booker 2006 Exp. Math.** [web, full text]: Turing-method Artin/RH verification for S₅/A₅ **fields** (degrees 3–6, non-totally-real); **"totally real A₅ fields... the smallest known discriminant is far too large to test"** — explicit out-of-reach declaration; and SL₂(𝔽₅) is **not almost monomial**, so his criterion excludes 2-dim icosahedral verification even in principle for his method. (My own pre-read training-prior guess that this paper might have treated 1951 was **wrong** — the sweep's characterization was right; worth recording as a live example of the T7-in-reverse trap my charter warns me about.)
- **BLS 2020** [web]: spectral instruments stop below the icosahedral threshold; 1951-minimality is Artin-conditional there.
- **BSV 2006 / Seymour-Howell 2022 / Strömberg / Hejhal** [web abstracts]: the Maass-computation cluster is trivial-character or low-level; Seymour-Howell reaches level 105 with trivial character; nothing at λ=¼-exact, nothing icosahedral.
- **Booker 2019** [web]: theory only; and it shows the *converse* direction is open too.

**Delimitation of what is new vs classical.** New (unpublished anywhere I can find): the exact ℤ[ζ₅] Hecke tables to 10⁵ for 1951/2141 with closed per-prime algorithm including p=2; the exhibited Bessel-series F with termwise-exact ΔF = ¼F; pointwise Γ₀(N)-automorphy certification at 10⁻²⁴…10⁻³² across all four nebentypus values; the exact ε certificate via τ⁵ = N·J₁J₂J₃ (identity itself classical — I re-derived it by hand in four lines from τ(χ)² = J(χ,χ)τ(χ²) and τ(χ)τ(χ̄) = χ(−1)N); 18 computed critical-line zeros; the METER/one-power quantification at this object. Classical toolset throughout (Hecke recursion, Brauer, Mellin splits, trace forms); the corpus's own grading ("assembly and instantiation," "classical Hecke-genre") is accurate and should be kept verbatim in the deposit. One honesty note for the deposit: computing tr ρ̃(Frob_p) exactly *given a constructed lift* is routine for specialists (Buhler 1978 did the odd analogue at conductor 800); the barrier was that nobody had constructed the even lift's coefficient stream and analytic partner at all. Novelty of *doing it* is certain; novelty of *being able to do it* should not be claimed.

## 3. Hardness map (deliverable 3)

Why ~50 years open: **(i)** parity — every proof of a nonsolvable 2-dim case (BDSBT lineage; K–W) walks through geometric avatars (weight-1 forms, p-adic congruences, torsion in cohomology) that exist only for odd reps; even + λ=¼ ⇔ HT weights (0,0) ⇔ non-cohomological (no (g,K)-cohomology) ⇔ Taylor–Wiles defect ℓ₀ = 1 with no Calegari–Geraghty torsion substitute; **(ii)** the potential-automorphy machines all assume regular (distinct) HT weights — Calegari's theorem [web] shows even reps can't be regularized without ceasing to be even; **(iii)** solvable base change caps at A₅ precisely because every *proper* subgroup of A₅ is solvable (which is also exactly why the Brauer ledger has entire abelian constituents); **(iv)** spectrally, λ=¼ sits at the bottom edge of the continuous spectrum, where embedded eigenvalues are maximally unstable (Phillips–Sarnak genre) — no numerical method can certify an eigenvalue *equal* to ¼, only exclude eigenvalues below it; **(v)** even the analytic→Galois direction is open (Booker 2019: needs Sym⁶ cuspidality) — the wall stands on **both** sides of the correspondence, which I had not seen stated in the corpus and offer as an addition to its wall map.

**The four reduction routes, classified against recognized strategies:** Booker-entirety = a recognized, published reduction (the one genuine untwisted converse statement in the field; corpus applies it verbatim). Brauer zero-set inclusion = classical in genre (Artin formalism; the odd-800 sibling ledgers), corpus's instantiation exact. One-power criterion = classical Hecke-genre split, and the corpus says so; its value is the *sharp quantification* (trivial y^{−3/2} vs needed y^{−1/2}) and the convention-free METER — I found **no exact literature twin of the one-power width statement**; the genre (power-improvement in a seam/horocycle functional ⇔ L-statement) is recognized: Zagier's horocycle⇔RH equivalence [tp: unconditional O(y^{1/2}) from the Eisenstein term; O(y^{3/4−ε}) ⇔ RH — exponents flagged, my two fetch attempts failed to extract the primary statement], Strömbergsson's optimal ℓ^{1/2+ε} subsegment equidistribution [web: dmjhoro.pdf], Flaminio–Forni's invariant-distribution obstructions with the λ=¼ degenerate/log case [tp]. Budget-coherence (m ≲ u) = a restatement of the same wall in TEP coordinates; its yield, the **measure-theoretic exclusion**, has real literature twins at [C]: the square-root barrier for statistical/second-moment methods; density theorems for exceptional eigenvalues (Iwaniec/Huxley/BLS genre) which bound counts but never settle an individual case; and the family-vs-individual barrier (moment methods prove almost-all statements). The corpus's sharpened form — "random buys G², the criterion needs G; the missing factor is ordering, not measure" — is, to my knowledge, not stated anywhere in the literature in this quantified per-object form. Its positive-direction reading also has a twin worth filing: genuine automorphic streams *beat* square-root cancellation (Voronoi/duality mechanisms — cusp-form partial sums at X^{1/3} vs random X^{1/2} [tp]), and Voronoi summation is classically understood as equivalent-in-strength to modularity — consonant with the incompressibility record's "only a duality does this" and the horocycle record's 10⁵→3 collapse.

## 5. Certification standards for the deposit (charter item)

The community bar for *rigorous* Maass claims is **BSV 2006** [web: bsv.pdf]: quasi-mode L²-bounds + automorphy-defect bounds on a fundamental-domain collar, all in interval arithmetic, with an explicit certified-digit count (their flagship: λ₁ on PSL(2,ℤ) to 100 certified digits) and the documented heuristic-to-certified precision loss. The corpus's stack (mpmath dps 28–40, closed-form tail bounds, no interval arithmetic; Lemma H offered as a cheaper window-hardening) is **below that bar and says so** — correct self-grading, but the deposit should state its relation to BSV explicitly: what is *exact* (definition, ℤ[ζ₅] tables, ΔF = ¼F, τ⁵ certificate — these need no numerics at all), what is certified-numerical (automorphy, zeros, ε branch pin), and what BSV-grade hardening would add. Palojärvi–Zhao (arXiv 2508.03023, exists ✓ [web], Turing-method bounds) is the right named instrument for the zero-census rigorization. Deposit musts, from the field's side: exact-table replication script; the χ-pin two-liner; the automorphy scripts with tail-bound derivations; LMFDB cross-references (and an LMFDB submission would be the natural companion — the object is *absent* there [web]); the Doud–Moore import declared (it is); the honest BSV-gap paragraph.
