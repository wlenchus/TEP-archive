## 1. The theorem, two lines [T; V 200/200]

**Finite factorization.** Every row-stochastic kernel K : Δ_m → Δ_n factors **exactly** as K = M ∘ E_K, where

- **E_K : Δ_m → Δ_{m·n}**, (E_K μ)_{(i,j)} = μ_i K_{ij} — a **congruent Markov embedding in Chentsov's literal sense**: outcome i refines into copies (i, j) with *fixed declared weights* K_{ij}, with Markov left inverse (i, j) ↦ i;
- **M : (i,j) ↦ j** — a **deterministic merge** (coarse-graining; the adjoint of conditioning on the merged algebra).

*Proof.* (M E_K μ)_j = Σ_i μ_i K_{ij} = (μK)_j. ∎
