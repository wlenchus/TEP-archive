# Part I — The arc: what the framework was, era by era

**QSF/UST (Q1 2025).** The program begins as a spectral-action attack on the Riemann zeta zeros, with a "Lorentz-like spectral metric ds² = C²dP² − P²dC²" over Persistence/Coherence coordinates — the seed of everything later called P/D duality and the gamma factor. This matters now for a reason no corpus document states: **the 2026 frontier (OT1, modular forms, the transmission functor) is a return to this starting point** — number theory via spectral/hyperbolic structure — with fifteen months of discipline acquired in between. More in Part VI.

**GSR (mid-2025).** An empirical scaling framework: quantum-critical-point fits (YbRh₂Si₂), temperature-as-uncertainty, exponent hunts. Its lasting exports were the Shannon–Hartley capacity identity — derived 2025-05-28 essentially in its final form, though the README misattributes it to 12-15 — and the budget vocabulary itself. Its core scaling hypothesis was eventually killed the right way: by its own preregistered comparative test (4-27: no advantage over standard critical scaling; linear wins on CeCoIn₅).

**IFFT (May–Aug 2025).** The maximal-ontology era: ψ_K/ψ_P fields, particles as solitons, claimed derivations of c, ℏ, G. Excised on 2025-11-16 in a remarkable three-document, same-day event: the Coherence-Entropy Lagrangian is proposed, killed ("Proton Decay Catastrophe," "critical category error"), and the excision formalized in the Framework Reconciliation — with the topological loop integral ∮J·dl = n installed as "the new, unassailable 'budget'." The excision was the corpus's first great act of subtraction. It was also — Part IV argues — its first over-broad one.

**TEP v0.5 (2025-08-23).** The founding document is *deliberately deflated* engineering mathematics: axioms A1–A3, Schur/Kron reduction, the positive-real DtN operator as the object of physics, passive holography, the budget metric, winding charges. The rigor culture is older than its reputation: the 9-25-25 canon scaffold already carries evidence tags and "Contribution Rules (to avoid 'vibe physics') … Kill metaphors; show math" — months before the tier ladder existed.

**December 2025.** The broadest synthesis (12-15 Comprehensive Documentation: Shannon–Hartley, the GMC correspondence, the vacuum sum rule) carries future-Tier-1 and future-Tier-5 material side by side with no internal grading — the strongest in-window argument for the tier system that followed. # Part II — The corrections engine: how this corpus fixes itself

**Channels, in order of historical yield.** (1) *Preregistered computation* — the highest-yield channel from April onward; by July every major kill is by a test the killed record itself specified. (2) *External adversarial review* — the 5-25 literature review remains the single most consequential document: it drove the priority demotions, the prior-art attributions (Anandan–Aharonov 1990), and the postmortem's governing rule. (3) *The errata instrument* (6-10 onward) — corrections as first-class objects with provenance. (4) *Author self-audit against interest* — the Liu timeline demotion is the corpus's flagship: Will supplied the evidence that demoted his own priority claim. (5) *Human meta-audit of the machine* — the decidability-gradient correction (07-26) and the training-prior disclosure convention: "every mid-course error yesterday was caught by machine or by Will, never by the author." (6) *Machine checks* — the mandatory non-flatness gates, d≥4 rules, and seed-pinned reproduction scripts of the 5-29 methods supplement.

**The great correction arcs, compressed.** The **budget** goes postulate (GSR) → conservation law (IFFT) → measurable partition (11-16) → "theorem" via assumed saturation (1-14, the era's central inflation) → tautology diagnosis (2-14) → *derived, twice, legitimately* (06-09 partition-of-unity; 07-19 Theorem P) — the February claim was wrong and its July successor is true, and the difference is exactly the difference between assuming saturation and deriving positivity. The **HBAR/silver-ratio** arc: born 2-1/2-2 → Tier-5-narrowed in April → provenance-corrected 6-1 → priority-demoted 6-10 → *relocated* in July ("the silver chain is the π/8 port," γ\* = 4sin²(π/8)) — the constant survived every framing built around it. The **Liu** arc is the cleanest three-act correction in the corpus: escalation (6-1 "forced-and-novel") → demotion on the author's own evidence (6-10) → closure (7-21: "Public priority: zero… content-distinctness: verified") → and then, in the frontier's best move, *adoption of the closure memo as the universal pricing template*. The **Hodge/DtN strong form** is falsified honestly (5-29: continuously tunable w vs fixed Betti number; the "harmonic" flow identically zero — "vacuity-by-zero") and sharply severed from the true Schur-holography reduction. The **kron-bug cascade** (7-04's "instrument-validated" 8000 exclusion, reversed 7-22/23) is the reference case for instrument-level malformed premises — and its reversal was *externally initiated* by a review's oldform argument before the corpus executed it, a nuance the period review's "zero non-self-filed reversals" smooths over.

**Failure modes of the correction process itself — in both directions.** The corpus is unusual in having documented its correctors' errors as carefully as its authors'. Enthusiasm-side: confirmation-shaped output, retrieval laundering ("fourth sighting"), compaction-frozen verdicts (A9: canon for one day because a compaction froze a mid-retraction), consolidation-prose drift. Skepticism-side — and this is the under-appreciated half: the deflation scare (genericity ≠ content-absence), *unfalsifiable tests* (a trivial-cancellation ratio and an accidentally flat connection that "could not have failed," caught only by verifying the test could fail), the absence-claim ratchet (thirteen "TEP lacks X" reversals), and the crowning case — **Addendum 2 (07-25): a four-review skeptical consensus had mis-cited canon in the *open* direction for a month** ("the absence-claim failure mode wearing skepticism's clothes"), and the hygiene machinery built against enthusiasm had no alarm for it. The Foundations Status block exists *because* the skeptics needed correcting.

**The propagation debt.** The corpus's most persistent defect is not error but latency: the ζ = 1/√2 erratum (ordered 5-25/5-26) still untracked at 7-25; the γ = 2/3 logging recommendation never executed; the Anandan–Aharonov repositioning still owed by the purity note; status vocabulary lagging the derivation record "by two to six weeks," capturing every reviewer including the frontier's own. As the period review puts it: "the corpus's derivation record is ahead of every summary of it." This report should be assumed subject to the same law.

# Part IV — Sidelined by malformed assumptions: the cases that answer your central question

These are the entries where the *sidelining premise itself* was later shown wrong — ranked by how cleanly the record demonstrates it. The first three are the adjudicator's "top malformed-premise revivals"; the rest complete the set, including two where the malformed premise ran in the inflationary direction and two process-level cases.

**1. The classical boundary-observable program (acoustic winding / S-parameters).** Sidelined on the winding-drift nulls of 1-15/1-16 — real nulls, honestly recorded. The malformation: the corpus's own 1-14 literature confrontation had *already published the repair* — complex-frequency excitation, where "the 'smearing' δ disappears when the probe's decay rate matches the system's loss rate" (verified verbatim), plus layer-peeling — and the notebooks never implemented it. The program died configured to fail, with its fix on file. Five months later, 6-20's delta mourns exactly these "under-emphasized measurable handles… the species of non-generic handle O2 has wanted." Nothing in the modern canon blocks a numerical revival; the direction memo's deposit-first rule gates only the bench-top half.

**2. The thermal/entropy sector, swept by Tier 5.5.** The gravity retirement's premise — "the framework does not derive Einstein's equations… This is reorganization, not derivation" — was *correct*. Its scope was not: it swept the separable thermal-operational results (FDT port thermometer, x²u entropy bookkeeping, sub-critical temperatures) and, most tellingly, V2's own Q1 falsifier: "A mismatch would establish that the identification is formal only" (verified verbatim) — written by the same document that ensured it never entered any queue. Both the February referee and V2 ranked Q1 decisive; the 07-19 canon still cites the Q1 fence; the computation has never been run. A retirement that was right about gravity silently orphaned the cheapest permanent adjudication the entropy sector ever had.

**3. The Grassmannian chiral-balance program.** Dropped by V2's triage note "not examined in depth during the consolidation" — an *administrative* truth that hardened into an evidential verdict downstream. The premise "unexamined ≈ unsupported" was false: the F₊=F₋ Klein-quadric algebra had been machine-verified to 10⁻¹⁷ by the 4-12 Rigor Check, was later re-verified [VR], and its α-curve sits exactly on the still-open Wick-seam join, with the corpus's own review naming the related I.8 spectral-reconstruction lead "genuine." The conflation of *ontology* with *dynamics* was not: the equations of motion, informational inertia, the E = M₀ sec A landscape, and the SO(2) Noether current were never refuted, only guilt-by-associated. The program then spent 2026 rebuilding a dynamics layer from scratch (Burden B, the metriplectic attempt, modular flow) — a rebuild that has still not reproduced what was excised. The modern metriplectic/monotone-metric machinery is precisely the safe re-derivation environment the 2025 version lacked.
