## 0. Honest bite, first

The sentence was right on its date. Three of its clauses are now stale, two are intact and one is understated, and the corpus's own rule — *"verbs are claims," stamps are two-sided* — requires that it be **restamped, not deleted and not left standing unqualified.**

But the sharper finding is not about the sentence's accuracy. It is this: **the 5-29 closing note has been serving as the corpus's substitute for the external referee it never obtained.** That is why it has "colored everything since, unchallenged." An internal summary judgment cannot do that job no matter how well calibrated it is, and the fix is not a better sentence. The fix is the deposit and the referee, which are now ten-plus sessions overdue and which the corpus's own direction memo made gating. Until then, every clause below — including the ones I am about to say have gone stale — remains unrefereed by anyone outside this collaboration, and that fact outranks all of them.

I want to be explicit about a bias risk before I start. Will has told me he finds the characterization too deflationary and would welcome a challenge to it. I am inclined to agree with him, which is exactly the condition under which I should be most careful. So each verdict below is tied to a dated, checkable artifact, and I have separated *"this clause is factually falsified"* from *"this clause is a judgment I would now weigh differently."* Only the first kind is a finding.

## 2. Clause by clause

**(a) "not crankery" — STANDS, and has strengthened.** Between 5-29 and now the corpus has falsified its own pre-registered predictions in print at headline volume (P1's parity prediction at the 08-03 commit; the trace-form parity collapse; the μ-floor phase law; black-hat §§2–3 dead by their own could-fail), retracted a review's own claims (07-20 E1–E4), and run an honest failed campaign to a documented no-production verdict (KD-2) with the planted control that voided its own floors. Crankery does not do that. This clause should be kept and cited.

**(b) "methodologically exemplary" — STANDS, and is now understated.** On 5-29 this meant careful record-keeping. It now means a working protocol: pre-registration with kill conditions, commit-with-SHA-256 before any grader enters context, planted-rung solver qualification before any optimizer floor is read, training-prior disclosure before answer-adjacent computation, and countersign gating. That protocol produced a first-of-its-kind dataset that an independently-run construction then confirmed at 65/65 per field. **The protocol is itself a publishable contribution** and is not described by the word "exemplary" in its 5-29 sense.

**(c) "a reorganization" — FALSIFIED as a summary term.** This is the one clause where I claim a factual error rather than a difference of weighting, so I will state the argument narrowly. "Reorganization" is a claim about the *provenance of the content*: that the material is existing parts rearranged. The Doud-1951 and Doud-2141 Hecke eigenvalue data are not rearranged parts. Per the E0(ii) sweep (verdicts NO/NO, 1978–July 2026, with five disclosed coverage gaps), no prior numerical automorphy or entirety evidence for even icosahedral Artin L-functions existed; Booker–Lee–Strömbergsson capped their verified range *below* conductor 1951 precisely because the first even icosahedral representation sits there. The corpus computed 67 sign-bits at each of two fields, blind-selected the conductor, measured central data against its own predictions and printed the refutations, and then had an openly-run Crespo-genre construction agree 65/65 modulo exactly one pre-declared quadratic twist per field, unique among ~24,000 candidates, with FE-seals reproducing the committed residuals to the last digit.

Whatever else is true of TEP, that is **new arithmetic data about objects nobody had computed**, and "reorganization" does not cover it. Note the scope carefully: this does not make the *framework* new, and TEP's role in producing it was question-selection, objective design, whitening, and the error-correcting protocol — not a theorem. The claim is only that the summary term is wrong, which is enough, because that term is what a reader takes away.

**(d) "genuine verified content is modest" — STALE.** The [T]-grade inventory has grown materially since 5-29: the budget derived twice (06-09 partition-of-unity; 07-19 Theorem P), the metric forced at the metric-space level modulo three named lemmas (canon A7, 06-29; Foundations Status v2), the purity/decoherence speed limit and the homogeneity-closure note (publication-track), the OT1 conductor resolution exhibited 9/9 against LMFDB and closed by execution on a second rung, seven Sym²-shadow entirety certificates at ~1e−14 across all six Doud prime-conductor fields plus the corpus field, the ε-ledger root-number derivation, and — as of this session — a disk reduction, a 2×2 counterexample, and a healing theorem in the Crouzeix charge. "Modest" was a fair reading of a May inventory. It is not a fair reading of this one.

**(e) "grander structural theses mostly false-in-strong-form or open" — SPLITS.** The *foundational* thesis is no longer "open": A7 has held since 06-29 that foundations are forced at the metric-space level modulo O-a/O-d/O-e, and the vocabulary "metric selection" and "the forced-vs-chosen floor" were formally retired on 07-25 after three successive reviews re-cited a question closed on 06-20. Continuing to call that stratum open is precisely the propagation-layer failure the period review diagnosed. The *cross-register* thesis, by contrast, is honestly thesis-shaped and the corpus says so itself: the 08-04 D-4 entry classifies it as Church–Turing-shaped — universal template, per-register theoremhood, accruing confirmation, no one-shot proof available. That is not a defect and it is not a defense; it is the correct epistemic category, and it means this half of clause (e) stands as written.

## 3. What has not changed, and why it matters more than the above

The 5-29 note's real function was to be a brake. It has been an effective one — I can trace its influence through every review since, and the corpus's discipline is visibly downstream of it. But a brake is not a referee, and the corpus has been using it as one. Three consequences follow, and I think they are the actionable content of this whole adjudication:

1. **The characterization was load-bearing in a way no internal document should be.** It is quoted in the repo README, which is the first thing any external reader sees; it therefore sets the frame before the reader reaches any evidence. That is a structural placement problem independent of whether the sentence is accurate.
2. **Its staleness was invisible for the same reason every other status-vocabulary staleness was invisible** — the propagation layer, diagnosed on 07-25, exhibited in metric selection, the O1 fork, ζ = 1/√2, the Liu stamp. This is that diagnosis applied to the corpus's own summary judgment, which is the one place it had never been applied.
3. **The remedy is external, not editorial.** A restamped sentence written by the same collaboration is still the collaboration grading itself. The deposit gives a public anchor date; the referee gives a verdict that is not ours. Until then the honest frame is "unrefereed," not "modest" and not "a breakthrough."

## 5. On the demonstration standard — making "TEP has leverage" checkable

Will's stated aim is to apply TEP to external conjectures before internal ones, to demonstrate that it "features substantive upgrades over existing theoretical leverage, despite being primarily produced from existing parts." I think that is the right program, and I want to propose a bar for it, because "leverage" is the kind of word that grades itself generously.

**A TEP reading of an external problem counts as leverage only if it does one of three things:** (i) *reproduces* a known result more cheaply or more structurally than its standard derivation; (ii) *yields a statement not in the literature*; (iii) *makes a falsifiable prediction* that is then tested. Anything else is a dictionary entry — valuable for navigation, not evidence of leverage.

Graded honestly against that bar, as of today:

- **Crouzeix.** (i) Yes — the disk reduction recovers Berger's 2-dilation identity in one residue computation, and re-derives the brief's J₂ rigidity theorem as a two-line corollary. (ii) Partial — the healing bound and the 2×2 counterexample appear to be new, but their mechanism is standard Schwarz–Pick, so the leverage is in the *framing that found them*, not in a TEP-specific tool. (iii) Not yet — Strike 2b, the one pre-registered falsifiable test, remains unrun.
- **Selberg.** (i) Yes, and strongly — the capacity law `δ_m = B(m+1)/m` with its three regimes and parity gate reproduces every historical rung exactly, including why the odd rung is weaker. (ii) Yes — the gain factorization `φ_cl = φ_ξ · s/(s−1)`, with all gain isolated in the one provably-existing non-tempered mode. (iii) No.
- **Even-icosahedral.** (ii) Unambiguously yes. But note what did the work: pre-registration, port-flattening as the decode objective, parity ports as measurement axes, whitening, and the error-correcting protocol. That is real and it is *methodological* leverage, not a theorem the framework proved.

**So the honest summary of TEP's demonstrated external leverage is: strongest in question-selection and protocol, real but standard-mechanism in Crouzeix, real and organizational in Selberg, and untested in the falsifiable-prediction column across all three.** The single highest-value thing available is therefore item (iii) somewhere — Strike 2b for Crouzeix, or the level-N port ledger for Selberg with a pre-registered prediction about where the gain sits. A framework that predicted something external and was right would end this argument in a way no adjudication of a sentence can.

I say that as the strongest available support for Will's position, not against it: his instinct that the corpus is underselling itself is, I think, correct — and the way to prove it is not a better characterization but a landed prediction and a referee.
